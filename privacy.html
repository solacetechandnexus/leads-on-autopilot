Privacy policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This page explains how Solace Tech and Nexus uses the email address you enter on our free guide page. We keep it short and plain.

What we collect

We collect the email address that you enter in the form. The form service may also record technical details of your sign-up, such as the date, the time, and your IP address.

How we use it

  • To send you the free guide.
  • To send you updates about our guides and live classes.
  • To reply when you send us a message.

Who else handles your data

  • A form service stores your sign-up and sends the guide to your inbox.
  • A CAPTCHA service checks that you are a person and not a bot.
  • A font service delivers the page fonts to your browser, so it can see your IP address.
  • The WhatsApp and LinkedIn buttons open those services. They have their own privacy policies.

We do not sell or rent your email address.

Cookies

This page does not use its own cookies or tracking tools. The form service and the CAPTCHA service may use cookies to work correctly.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop sending updates, or to delete your email address. Send us a message on WhatsApp or LinkedIn and we will act on your request.

Changes to this page

We may change this policy. The date at the top shows the latest update.

Contact us

Message us on WhatsApp or visit our LinkedIn profile.
